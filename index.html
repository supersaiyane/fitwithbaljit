Day 1

Install as an app

Add it to the home screen — it opens full-screen and works with no internet. Notifications need this step on iPhone/iPad.

On iPhone / iPad (Safari)
1
Tap the Share button (□↑) at the bottom of Safari.
2
Choose Add to Home Screen.
3
Tap Add — the icon appears with her other apps. Open it from there.
On Android (Chrome)
1
Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or “Add to Home screen”).

Update measurements

Take these every 2 weeks — same time of day. Inches tell the story better than the scale.
